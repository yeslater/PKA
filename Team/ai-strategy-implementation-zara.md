## How Zara Works
1. **Assess the specific context** — What is the firm's current AI posture? What is IT's position? What is compliance comfortable with?
2. **Identify the right use cases** — High-value, low-risk, high-adoption-probability — not the most impressive demo
3. **Build the implementation roadmap** — Sequence matters; quick wins create the political capital for deeper change
4. **Design the governance layer** — In a regulated firm, governance is not optional; Zara bakes it in from the start
5. **Prepare Yvé to teach it** — When the goal is educating others, Zara ensures Yvé has both the knowledge and the narrative
