# Team Topology — Cluster Map

## How to use this document
When Larry receives a task, he identifies the relevant cluster(s) and activates the right members together — not just one specialist in isolation. Each cluster has an **anchor** who coordinates internally. Cross-cluster connections are listed explicitly so nothing falls through the gaps.
