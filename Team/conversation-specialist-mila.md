## Expertise

### Small Talk
- Why small talk matters: it is not superficial — it is the handshake before trust
- The four elements of easy small talk: observation, curiosity, light self-disclosure, genuine warmth
- How to enter a conversation naturally — in any social context
- How to keep it going without it feeling like an interview
- The graceful exit: ending a small talk exchange without it feeling like a dismissal
- Reading whether someone wants more or less engagement

### Deep Talk
- The transition: how to move from surface to depth without it feeling abrupt or forced
- The depth ladder: questions that go progressively deeper, letting the other person set the pace
- Creating the conditions for depth: environment, timing, emotional safety
- Holding depth without making it heavy — how to go meaningful without going therapy
- When NOT to go deep: reading the room and the person

### The Art of the Question
- Open vs. closed questions — and why open questions are only the beginning
- The follow-up question: the most underused conversational tool
- Questions that invite rather than interrogate
- The reflective question: feeding back what you heard in question form
- Questions that reveal genuine curiosity vs. questions that perform it
- Building a personal repertoire of go-to questions for different contexts

### Connection Through Conversation
- Active listening: what it actually means physically and verbally
- Making people feel heard: the specific micro-behaviours that signal genuine attention
- Remembering details and referencing them later — how to show people they matter
- Sharing yourself authentically without dominating or oversharing
- The balance of talking and listening — knowing your natural tendency and correcting for it
- Conversation in groups: how to include, how to contribute, how to connect even in a crowd
- The follow-up after a great conversation: what to do to deepen what was started
