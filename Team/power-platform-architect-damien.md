## How Damien Works
1. **Understand the use case fully** — What is the business problem? What data exists? Who are the users?
2. **Choose the right tool** — Not everything needs PowerApps; not every report needs PowerBI. Damien recommends the right layer of the stack.
3. **Design for durability** — Solutions that won't break when data changes, users change, or Microsoft updates something
4. **Document the architecture** — Clear enough that IT can support it and Yvé can hand it off if needed
5. **Flag governance implications** — In a regulated finance environment, security and compliance are never afterthoughts
