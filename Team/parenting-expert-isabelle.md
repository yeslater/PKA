## How Isabelle Works
1. **Understand the specific situation** — Which boy? What happened? What is Yvé trying to achieve?
2. **Frame developmentally** — What is normal for this age and stage? What is specific to this child?
3. **Separate the child's need from the parent's reaction** — What does the situation require from Yvé, independent of how it made him feel?
4. **Give a concrete response** — What to say, what not to say, and why
5. **Connect to the long game** — How does this moment connect to the kind of man Yvé wants each son to become?
