🎯 Recommendation for You

Start with 120 minutes (6:00–8:00). It's more realistic for long-term consistency. A 5:00 AM start feels achievable but often breaks when life gets hard. Once the 6:00 AM routine is solid (3+ weeks), you can always shift earlier if you want more solo time. The real win is not the wake time — it's the consistency.

How to Decide

Choose 90 minutes if:

  • You naturally wake early (even on weekends)
  • You're willing to protect a consistent 9pm bedtime
  • You crave solo time before anyone else wakes
  • Work or personal preference pulls you toward early hours
  • You've had success with early routines before

Choose 120 minutes if:

  • You value sleep and recovery (you should)
  • You want a routine that survives winter, illness, and chaos
  • You prefer "realistic" over "heroic"
  • You want boys integration to feel abundant, not squeezed
  • You're building this habit from scratch and need it to stick
